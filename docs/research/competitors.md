# David’s Barber Shop — competitor and price research

**Checked: 2026-10-22. Currency: USD.** Subject: David’s Barber Shop, 233 East 84th Street, New York, NY 10028; father-and-son neighborhood shop since 1972.

## Executive recommendation

**Recommended comparison amount: 4500 cents ($45), not an unqualified “$55 elsewhere in Manhattan.”**

Suggested substantiated copy, subject to confirming David’s offer:

> **$30 haircut at David’s. The median advertised standard haircut in our eight-shop Upper East Side comparison was $45.** Prices checked October 22, 2026; participating shops and sources linked below.

A shorter headline can say **“$30 here. $45 median in our UES comparison.”** Keep the sample qualification and accessible source note next to the comparison; do not turn this into “all other barbers charge $45,” “Manhattan average,” or “save $25.”

**One-line source note:** Median of eight UES competitors’ published standard haircut prices ($40–$65), checked 2026-10-22; shop websites and booking menus documented in U1–U8 below.

**Launch blocker:** David’s old website advertises Hair Cut **$25** and Shampoo and Cut **$30**, while its currently bookable Booksy listing advertises Haircut **$35**. The proposed **$30 haircut is a new offer, not the verified current Booksy price**. Confirm the exact service, eligibility, expiry, and barber availability with the owner and make the booking destination honor it before running ads. A second, older Booksy listing also exists. See the David’s baseline below.

## Method, scope, and limitations

- Web search discovered shops, official sites, current service menus, booking destinations, and reputation evidence. Fetched the shops’ own sites and linked booking pages rather than treating search snippets or historical editorial prices as current prices.
- **17 competitors:** eight UES shops and nine elsewhere in NYC, of which seven are in Manhattan and two are in Brooklyn/Queens. David’s is a separate baseline and is **not counted** as a competitor or in any median.
- UES inclusion: roughly East 59th–East 96th Streets, Fifth Avenue–East River. Addresses are included to distinguish similarly named businesses. Chains receive one observation, not one per branch. Prices for Brooklyn/Queens are excluded from the Manhattan calculation.
- “Well-regarded” is a research-selection criterion, not an objective quality award: the wider sample has independent editorial coverage or substantial positive booking-platform reviews. The basis is linked below.
- Comparison measure: one publicly displayed **standard adult haircut** amount per shop, using the lower endpoint of a published range or “from” price. Excluded buzz-only cuts, apprenticeships, maintenance, children/seniors, bundles, new-client discounts, and consultation packages. Lower endpoints are conservative and are **not a guarantee every barber charges that price**.
- Where the website and a location-specific booking menu disagree, prefer the **regular, undiscounted booking price** and disclose both. Thus Premium contributes $39.95, not the website’s $35.95 or its temporary $37.95 booking offer. For Pall Mall, $54 is the lower standard-cut price among the two barber profiles actually checked, not a claim to have checked every independent barber.
- Medians are of sampled **entry prices**, not a customer-weighted market survey. Price range means minimum/maximum of those entry observations, not the most expensive possible service. Samples are purposive, small, and price-disclosure-biased. No statistical inference to every Manhattan barbershop is justified.
- All observations below carry the access date **2026-10-22**, including tables and booking links. Access date is not a promise that the price will remain unchanged. Recheck before launch and monthly while a comparison ad runs.
- **“not found” means not verified in the pages inspected**, not that a feature does not exist. We do not turn absence into “no.” For gift cards/memberships, **y** requires affirmative evidence; **n** would require an explicit negative statement (none verified). Generic platform upsells do not prove a shop actually sells a membership.
- Hero classification uses extracted images, text, and public HTML video markup; no screenshots or video playback testing. “Video” means implemented video, not verified playback. Gallery means a visible collection of shop/work photos, not proof that every photo is an authentic client result.
- Local SEO: inspected public homepage HTML for JSON-LD types and extracted address/hours, Maps/review links, and location pages. **Presence is not schema validation or evidence that a GBP is claimed.** “Maps” and “GBP/review link” are distinguished where possible. Unverified schema/links are marked not found. No rankings, GBP admin, or search-console access was available.
- Booking pages sometimes return only a shell, cookie screen, reCAPTCHA, or location selector. Those failures are recorded rather than inventing prices or platform features.
