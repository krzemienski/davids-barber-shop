# David's Barber Shop — brand guide

**Status: draft, awaiting owner sign-off.** Nothing here is final until the owner approves it; the logo, vinyl and print items in particular wait on measurements and the owner's branding photo.

## Who we are

A father-and-son barbershop at **233 East 84th Street**, between Second and Third Avenue on the Upper East Side, cutting hair since **1972**. Regulars travel in from Queens, Long Island and East Harlem to keep their barber. A classic cut is **$30**.

**Positioning line:** *Manhattan quality, $30 a cut.*

## Voice

- Plain, warm, neighbourly; short sentences. Talk like the barber, not like a spa.
- Lead with the facts customers act on: price, address, hours, "Closed Saturdays", how to book.
- Proof comes from real customers, quoted word for word with first name and last initial. Never edit a review's meaning, never invent one.
- Avoid: "luxury", "elevated", "experience" as a noun, ethnic or religious descriptors of the shop, barbers or clients, celebrity names without recorded written consent, superlatives we cannot back ("best in NYC" only inside a customer's own quote).
- Comparisons only with a dated source: "vs. $45 median at nearby UES shops" plus the source footnote (see `docs/research/competitors.md`).

## Colour

Dark, warm shop interior: black-brown base, brass and gold fittings, oxblood leather, bone-white towels.

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0D0B0A` | Page background, text on gold buttons |
| Card | `#171512` | Panels, cards, admin surfaces |
| Bone | `#ECE6DA` | Primary text, headings |
| Gold | `#C8A464` | Primary buttons, prices, links, focus rings, accents |
| Bright gold / brass | `#C3A617` | Small highlights only (chart accent, hover glints). Never body text. |
| Oxblood | `#912731` | "Closed Saturdays", warnings, the red stripe in the pole rule |

Contrast: bone on ink (~16:1), gold on ink (~8:1) and ink on gold all pass WCAG AA for body text. Keep bright gold to accents, not paragraphs.

## Type

| Role | Family | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display / headings / prices | **Playfair Display** (400–700, italic for the accent word) | Pattern: plain word + gold italic word — "Find the *chair.*", "Services *& prices.*" |
| Body / UI / buttons | **Poppins** (300–700) | Buttons semibold, never all caps except eyebrows |
| Hours, phone, data | **JetBrains Mono** (400–600) | Hours tables, admin numbers, timestamps |

Eyebrows: Poppins 11–12 px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.25–0.35em, gold, with a short gold rule before them.

## Graphic devices

- **Pole rule:** a thin diagonal stripe of gold / bone / oxblood, the barber pole flattened into a divider. Used between hero and content and above the footer. Static when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- **Tile floor:** a subtle black-and-charcoal diamond pattern, from the shop's floor. Used behind monogram cards and the staff sign-in.
- **Monogram card:** when a barber has no photo, show their initials in gold Playfair on the tile floor. Never use stock faces.
- **Grain:** a faint film-grain overlay on dark surfaces.

## Logo

Working mark: a gold circle with a "D" and a razor stroke, with the wordmark "David's" in Playfair and "BARBER SHOP · 1972" in spaced Poppins caps. Files: `artifacts/davids-barbershop/public/logo.svg`, `favicon.svg`.

Open items before final artwork: owner sign-off, the owner's branding photo, and clarification of 1972 vs. the "84" used in the old domain.

## Photography and video

- Real shop, real tools, real people. Warm tungsten light, deep shadows, gold highlights.
- The hero video plays muted, loops, has a visible pause button, and is replaced by a still poster when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- Until real barber portraits exist, use the monogram card rather than generic photos.
