- Copy may only state facts the owner or verbatim reviews support. Reviews are quoted word for word; one review mentioning $35 is deliberately excluded.
- Known conflicts the owner must resolve: offer $30 vs Booksy $35 vs old site $25; site roster David/Sean vs Booksy listing showing only Michael; 1972 vs the "84" in the old domain.
- Comparison claim ($45 median of 8 UES shops, checked 2026-10-22) stays off until owner approval.
- Celebrity names (from the old site) are never published without recorded consent.

**Why:** the brief demands compliance (no unverified claims, no ethnic/religious labels, consent for celebrities); review rounds removed several invented lines.
**How to apply:** any copy, seed data, ad text or landing-page change.
