# David's Barber Shop: hero background loop

Purpose: a text-free, 16 s seamless background loop for the website hero. The site's HTML headline sits over the left ~45% under dark gradients, so every subject and every motion accent stays in the right 55% of the frame. The site shows the video with object-position 70% center, so the focus sits around x 62-80%.

Treatment: The shop after hours, warm under amber light. Palette: ink #0D0B09, gold #C8A464, oxblood #91263A, bone #EAE3D5. There is no on-screen type because the website supplies it. The hero is the shop's own photography. Motion: a slow camera push and drift on each plate; a gold-and-oxblood diagonal barber-pole stripe band sweeps across each cut (compound cut: stripe wipe plus scale/blur crossfade); ambient steam rises on the right. Arc: chair, tools, room, towel, then back to the chair. Payoff: shot 4 ends on the same chair framing as shot 1, so the loop closes without a visible cut.

Beat density: steam wisps begin every 0.6 s, a glint or hairline accent sounds within each 1 s, and the camera never stops moving.
